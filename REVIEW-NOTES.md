# Publication Review Notes

Reviewed and published 22 October 2026.

- Original PDFs/XLSX are unchanged; reviewed Markdown separates plans, reports and confirmed visual evidence.
- The share-link PDF was visually reviewed on all four pages. The screenshot supports a visible error and HTTP500, not proof of a server-process crash. Its BDD ticket text is small; no scenario execution count is inferred.
- The RACI is a simplified exercise; the database row has no explicit accountable party.
- Coverage categories are planned scope, not executed cases. Synthetic representative data is sufficient for usability testing.
- The mapping workbook's HTTP500 value is a reported defect outcome, not the desired result. Its claim that the data layer was not reached is not independently established.
- Workbook route names and localhost ports belong to the respective lab exercises. They may differ from later account-update or checkout labs.
- No new live app tests were performed for this publication. Further regression, RCA, SQL, compatibility and AI exercises are intentionally outside this first batch.
