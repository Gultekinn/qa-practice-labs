# Project Falcon quality playbook

A completed coursework exercise outlining QA and QC responsibilities and three guiding testing principles. Published on **25 October 2026**; the source does not specify a completion date.

## Quality Assurance: improving the process

- Establish and improve quality guidelines and testing practices.
- Review requirements and designs early to identify ambiguity and missing information.
- Check whether agreed development and review practices are followed.
- Use sprint retrospectives to suggest workflow improvements.

## Quality Control: evaluating the product

- Design and execute tests for implemented features.
- Document defects and retest fixes when available.
- Run regression checks after changes.
- Compare product behavior with the agreed requirements.

These are proposed responsibilities in the exercise, not claims of having performed them in an employed QA role. The process/product distinction is a learning summary; testing can also evaluate requirements and intermediate work, rather than waiting for a completely finished product.

## Guiding principles

| Principle | Application proposed in the submission |
| --- | --- |
| Early testing | Review requirements early so unclear or missing details can be addressed before they become larger problems. |
| Defect clustering | Give additional attention to areas likely to contain more defects. |
| Exhaustive testing is impossible | Prioritize important scenarios rather than attempting every possible combination. |

Review clarification: defect clustering does not justify ignoring other modules. Use observed defect patterns together with business risk when selecting coverage; this exercise supplies no defect dataset proving a cluster.
